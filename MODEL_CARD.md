# Model Card

## Model family

LAM-JEPA is a latent-action predictive architecture designed for educational reasoning and tutoring research.

## Intended use

- reasoning benchmarks
- adaptive tutoring research
- misconception detection
- curriculum selection experiments
- explanation and intervention selection

## Out of scope

- high-stakes educational placement without human oversight
- clinical or mental-health assessment
- deployment without benchmark validation and safety review

## Training data

The repo ships synthetic and connector-ready dataset formats. It does not claim to include proprietary external datasets.

## Evaluation

Use the benchmark suite, seed sweeps, and calibration/OOD summaries in this repo before any external claims.
